Hotel License & Permit Requirements
 
Mandatory Licenses:
- Hotel Registration License from Tourism Department
- Trade License from Municipal Corporation
- GST Registration
- Shop & Establishment License
- Health Trade License (if serving food)
- Liquor License (if applicable)
 
Documents to Verify:
- Business PAN Card
- Owner Aadhaar & Photo ID
- Property Ownership/Tenancy Proof
- Building Stability Certificate
- Pollution Control Board NOC (if required)
 
All licenses must be valid and renewed on time.
