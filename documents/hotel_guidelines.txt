Hotel Business Operation Guidelines
 
1. All hotels must maintain valid Fire Safety Certificate.
2. Staff must be trained in emergency evacuation procedures.
3. Rooms must meet minimum hygiene and sanitation standards.
4. Proper guest registration and ID verification is mandatory.
5. Food and beverage services require separate FSSAI license if applicable.
6. CCTV coverage in common areas is recommended.
7. Regular pest control and maintenance logs must be maintained.
8. Compliance with local tourism department regulations is mandatory.
 
Key Compliance: Safety, Hygiene, Guest Security.
