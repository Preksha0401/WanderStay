Fire Safety Requirements for Hotels
 
1. Valid Fire NOC (No Objection Certificate) must be present.
2. Fire extinguishers must be placed on every floor and serviced regularly.
3. Emergency exits and staircases must remain unobstructed.
4. Smoke detectors and fire alarms must be functional.
5. Staff training records for fire drills must be available.
6. Electrical wiring must follow safety standards.
7. Flammable materials must be stored safely.
8. Evacuation plan must be displayed in all rooms and common areas.
 
Non-compliance with fire safety can lead to immediate closure.
