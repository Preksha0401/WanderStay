Business License Verification Guidelines

Key Principles for Verification (based on official US government practices from SBA and state resources):

1. Business Name / Legal Name must be present and match official records.
   - Verify against Secretary of State databases or equivalent.

2. License Number / Registration Number must be present.
   - Cross-check with issuing authority's verification portal.

3. Issue Date should be available.
   - Ensures the license is current and not fabricated.

4. Expiry Date should be available.
   - If expiry date is missing or passed, manual verification or rejection is required.
   - Always check renewal status via official channels.

5. Physical Address must be present and verifiable.
   - Confirm against business registration and utility/tax records if possible.

6. Additional Mandatory Fields depending on jurisdiction:
   - EIN/Tax ID (for most businesses)
   - Owner/Principal information
   - Business Type/Entity Structure (LLC, Corp, etc.)
   - Industry-specific permits if applicable.

7. Verification Process:
   - Use official government websites to validate (e.g., Secretary of State business search, SBA resources).
   - If more than two mandatory fields are missing or inconsistent, recommend rejection.
   - If all core mandatory fields are present and verifiable, recommend provisional approval pending admin review.

8. AI should only assist the admin with data extraction, cross-checking suggestions, and flagging inconsistencies.
   - Final approval must always be done by a human admin to ensure compliance and accountability.

Important Notes:
- Requirements vary by state, county, and city. Always consult jurisdiction-specific resources.
- Never rely solely on submitted documents; cross-reference with official public records.

Sources and Links for Verification:
- U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA): https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits
- General business lookup examples: Washington DOR Business Lookup, Florida MyFloridaLicense, etc.
- Secretary of State websites for each state (searchable via https://www.sos.state.[state-abbrev].us/ or similar).
- For detailed guides: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/register-your-business

This knowledge base should be updated as regulations change. Always prioritize official government sources for the most current information.
